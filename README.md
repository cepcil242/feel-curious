`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a small mystery: a lighthouse on an empty coast that still flashes a pattern every night at 3:14, forty years after the last ship passed, its keeper gone and its logbook ending mid-sentence. Concrete details, a question with no answer, meant to leave the reader wanting to know more.
